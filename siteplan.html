Bakyala Basketball - Site Plan

Name: Atukunda Jacoba

1. Site Name

Bakyala Basketball - A website for Bakyala basketball teams, players, fixtures and news in Uganda.

2. Site Purpose

To share information about Bakyala basketball teams, show star players, list upcoming fixtures, and keep fans updated with latest news.

3. Scenarios

  • When is the next Bakyala basketball game and where will it be played?
  • Who are the star players and what teams do they play for?
  • How can I join or contact a Bakyala basketball team?

4. Color Schema

Dark Blue #1a3a5c - Headings and Header

Red #c0392b - Accents and Buttons

White and Light Grey - Background and Text

5. Typography

Headings: Arial Black / Arial Bold
Body: Arial, sans-serif
